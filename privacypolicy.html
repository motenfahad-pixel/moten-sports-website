Our Commitment

Moten Sports Construction (trading as MDS – Moten Designs) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or submit an inquiry.

When you use our website or submit a quote request, we may collect:

  • Full Name

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Communication via WhatsApp & Phone

By submitting your information through our website, you consent to being contacted by Moten Sports Construction via:

This communication is strictly related to your inquiry, project consultation, or relevant service updates.

We may share your information only with:

5. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to fulfill your inquiry, provide services, and comply with legal requirements.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

You may request to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data

To do so, contact us at: hello@motenbuilds.com

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The updated version will always be posted on this page.
