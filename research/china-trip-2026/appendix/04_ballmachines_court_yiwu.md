## 1.3 White-label hardware + ScoreX proprietary app: assessment

**Key finding:** No supplier publicly offers BLE protocol documentation, an SDK or an API. The commodity Chinese machines typically use a Bluetooth 4.0 module talking UART to a motor controller (for example, the YS launcher: "built-in Bluetooth 4.0 module, self-developed app, 30 m range"). This architecture is easy to integrate **if the factory hands over the command table**. Sources: [Harvey Norman YS launcher](https://www.harveynorman.com.au/ys-automatic-portable-tennis-ball-machine.html), [USU senior project reference design](https://engineering.usu.edu/files/pdfs/senior-design/fall2021-spring2022/ece/pickleball-machine.pdf).

**Shortlist for the white-label + app model:**
1. **Siboasi / Skyegle (Dongguan):** Widest range (tennis, padel, pickleball, badminton, football, basketball), stated OEM, existing app hardware, CE/RoHS claims. Make this the first visit.
2. **DK Sportbot (Dongguan):** Smaller company with its own app and a tennis + padel focus. A smaller firm may be more willing to share its protocol.
3. **Xunyun / Skylin (Dongguan):** Cheapest padel and pickleball units. Good for a prototype integration where ScoreX reverse-engineers or requests the BLE commands at low cost.
4. **Pongbot / Tenniix:** Premium AI. Approach as ODM or tech partners, not commodity white-label.

**Questions to ask every supplier (put them in writing in the RFQ):**
- Will you provide the **BLE GATT service/characteristic map and command protocol** (speed, spin, angle, oscillation, interval, drill sequences, battery, ball-count telemetry) under NDA?
- Can you flash **custom firmware** or allow OTA through our app? Who owns the firmware IP? Can we get the source or a binary escrow?
- Is the **Wi-Fi option** available (ESP32-class) so the court camera and scoreboard network can drive the machine?
- What are the costs for **custom enclosure colour, logo and packaging**? What are the MOQ and mould fee for a custom shell?
- Which certifications exist for the battery and radio: **CE-RED, FCC ID, UN38.3, MSDS, RoHS**? These matter for export and air freight of lithium packs.
- What are the warranty terms and the spare-parts supply (wheels, motors, boards)? What is the lead time?

## 2.2 Notes by category

**Scoreboards (ScoreX core):**
- ScoreX already designs scoreboards. China's value lies in **sub-assemblies**: 7-segment LED digit modules, P2.5–P10 outdoor/indoor LED modules, RF/BLE remote keypads, enclosures and power supplies. Shenzhen (Bao'an/Longhua) is the LED module hub. **[UNVERIFIED – background knowledge]**
- The big LED brands (Absen, Unilumin, Leyard, Liantronics) suit venue video walls and perimeter boards. They are poorly suited to small padel scoreboards. Mid-tier Shenzhen module makers and dedicated scoreboard OEMs will quote lower MOQs.
- **Timing problem:** Electronics and lighting are in **Phase 1 (Oct 15–19)**, which has already started. Plan **Shenzhen factory visits** in the Oct 20–22 or Oct 28–30 gaps between phases.

**Court accessories (MDS):**
- Phase 3 (Sports & Recreation) and Phase 2 (building materials) cover nets, posts, umpire chairs, artificial turf and court lighting.
- Nantong (Jiangsu) is the main fitness and sports-equipment cluster. Ironman and Winner are long-standing Canton exhibitors.

**Rackets and paddles:**
- **Guangzhou Jusain** is local to the fair and the easiest visit.
- **Hangzhou Shuoshuo Guangyi** covers padel and pickleball and is near Yiwu and Hangzhou, so it could be combined with an East China leg.

**Smart sensors:**
- There is limited white-label sensor supply evidence. Coollang is a possible partner. ScoreX's camera-based analytics may make racket sensors unnecessary.

## 3.1 What Yiwu offers

- **Yiwu International Trade City (Futian Market)**, Zhejiang, is the world's largest small-commodities wholesale market, with five main districts and tens of thousands of booths. **[UNVERIFIED – background knowledge]**
- **Sports goods:** Located mainly in **District 3** (stationery, sports goods, cosmetics, glasses), with some fitness and outdoor items in District 4/5. **[UNVERIFIED – background knowledge; confirm floor on arrival or via the Yiwu market map]**
- **Typical offer:**
  - Badminton rackets and shuttles, table-tennis bats, low-end tennis and padel rackets, pickleball paddle sets (fiberglass/wood).
  - Balls, nets, grips, overgrips, wristbands, bags.
  - Resistance bands, yoga mats, dumbbells and small gym accessories.
  - Whistles, cones, ladders, generic scoreboard flip-charts.
- **Quality and model:** Most booths are **traders or the showrooms of small workshops**. Products are generic catalogue items at the low to mid quality tier.
  - MOQs are low, often per carton or a few hundred pieces, and mixed-SKU consolidation is easy. **[UNVERIFIED – background knowledge]**
  - Weak on engineering, certifications, carbon-fiber racket quality, electronics, firmware and OEM tooling.
- **Electronics:** Not a meaningful source for ScoreX electronics (ball machines, scoreboards, cameras). Those come from Dongguan and Shenzhen.

## 3.3 Getting there **[UNVERIFIED – background knowledge; check 12306/Trip.com]**

- **Shanghai Hongqiao → Yiwu:** G-train, about 1.5–2 h, frequent departures.
- **Hangzhou East → Yiwu:** About 30–45 min.
- **Guangzhou South → Yiwu:** Through G-trains run about 6.5–8 h. Flying is usually faster.
- **Guangzhou → Yiwu Airport (YIW):** Domestic flights of about 2 h, several daily. Alternatively fly to Hangzhou (HGH) and take the train.

## 3.4 Recommendation: **NO, not a dedicated Yiwu trip. Optional half-day only if the itinerary already passes through Shanghai or Hangzhou.**

**Reasoning:**
1. ScoreX's strategic items (ball machines with an app, scoreboards, camera electronics) are absent from Yiwu. They are concentrated in Dongguan and Shenzhen, 1–2 h from the Canton Fair venue.
2. MDS's court items, the branded racket/paddle OEM and the fitness gear are better sourced factory-direct (Guangzhou, Nantong, Hangzhou, Xiamen) or at Canton Phase 3, where those same factories exhibit.
3. Yiwu's strengths (low MOQ, mixed SKUs, cheap generic accessories) suit **merchandising and add-on retail items**. They can be ordered later through a Yiwu sourcing agent without travelling. Reaching Yiwu from Guangzhou costs a full day each way by rail, or about 0.5–1 day by air.
4. **Better use of the Oct 28–30 gap:** Dongguan (ball machines) and Shenzhen (LED/scoreboard modules, sensors) factory visits.
