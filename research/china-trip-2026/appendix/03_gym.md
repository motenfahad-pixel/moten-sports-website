# 03 — Commercial Gym Equipment Suppliers (China) for MDS Karachi Tender

Research date: 2026-10-15. Prepared for MDS (Karachi) China sourcing trip: 140th Canton Fair Phase 3 plus factory visits.

## 0. How this was researched, and its limits (read first)

- **Method:** WebSearch only. WebFetch could not resolve any host from this sandbox (DNS failures on cantonfair.org.cn, company sites, chinadaily, wikipedia), so **no official company website or Canton Fair exhibitor page was opened directly**. Every fact below comes from search-result summaries of the URLs cited.
- **Search budget:** the shared WebSearch budget for the session ran out partway through. Some planned checks were not done, including Shuhua's Middle East distributors, Shandong EM, Lingdong, Xinrui, Rongtai, Kangqiao, Bodyfit, Ningjin Mike, Nantong Leeton and Teyin, Xiamen Kingdom, Ganas, BH, and Jialifu lockers.
- **Source quality:** most company facts are **self-reported** on Made-in-China, B2USA, SourceReady, FreshDi or Epic Sourcing listings, or come from **fragfitty.de FIBO 2026 trade-show write-ups**. The stronger sources are China Daily or Xinhua for Ningjin and DHZ, stock-exchange listings for Impulse and Shuhua, and Johnson's own blog. Yanrefitness.com (YR Fitness) is a competitor's marketing site, so its rankings are biased.
- **Canton Fair booth numbers:** **None were found for any supplier. None are given, and none should be assumed.** The only evidenced Canton Fair link is Shandong Relax Health Industry (136th session, "many sessions"; no booth number). FIBO 2026 (Cologne) stand numbers are given only where a source states them, and they are **not** Canton Fair booths.
- **Unconfirmed names (no search hits):** Shandong Kangqiao, Bodyfit, EM Fitness, Lingdong, Xinrui, Ningjin "Mike", Nantong Leeton, Nantong Teyin, Jiangsu Haili, "Shandong Lizhiyou", "Ruibu", "Longzhiyuan". These were not found. They may be misspellings or trade names, so treat them as unverified.

## 2. Cluster context: Ningjin County (Dezhou, Shandong)

- About 2,509 registered fitness enterprises, **20 of them large-scale**. 2024 output was RMB 13.9 bn (Xinhua via China Daily). The county claims more than 70% of China's domestic fitness-equipment market, exports to 170+ countries, and "3 in 10 commercial fitness machines sold worldwide" (local-government figure, unverified).
  - https://subsites.chinadaily.com.cn/shandong/2025-07/31/c_1113586.htm
  - https://investinchina.chinaservicesinfo.com/s/202608/31/WS6a94ef70498e36855033f8e1/ningjins-fitness-equipment-takes-on-global-markets.html
  - https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202504/04/content_WS67efdc25c6d0868f4e8f1709.html
  - https://equalocean.com/news/2023092520245-chinas-international-city-fitness-equipment-production-base-ningjin-county
- **Main finding:** Ningjin is where you can source the whole commercial package (cardio, selectorized and plate-loaded strength, racks and rigs, benches, plus some Pilates) in a single 2–3 day visit.

## 7. Questions to put to every factory

1. EN ISO 20957 test reports (Parts 1, 2 and 6 for treadmills), CE Declaration of Conformity (machinery directive), ISO 9001 certificate number, and the issuing body (TÜV, SGS, etc.).
2. Middle East and South Asia project references, and any existing Pakistan dealer or territory exclusivity.
3. Warranty (frame, parts, electronics, wear items), a spare-parts kit of 2–5% of order value, and technician training or remote support.
4. Treadmill motor rating (continuous HP), maximum user weight, and voltage (230 V / 50 Hz for Pakistan).
5. Weight-stack tolerance, cable spec and upholstery; free-weight tolerance (±%) and odour (CPU vs rubber).
6. MOQ, packing (wooden crate vs carton), 40HQ loading plan, lead time, and Incoterms (FOB Qingdao or Tianjin for Shandong goods).
