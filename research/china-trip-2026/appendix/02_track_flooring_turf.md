## 0. Read this first: method, limits and confidence

- **Tool limits during this research.** WebSearch worked until the session's shared search budget ran out. WebFetch and direct HTTP were blocked in this environment: DNS/proxy refused worldathletics.org, made-in-china.com, fiba.basketball, tx1993.com and others. So **no company website or official PDF was opened directly**. Every fact below comes from search-engine snippets of the cited pages. Each URL is the page the snippet came from.
- **Certification claims are mostly self-reported.** They come from supplier or marketplace pages. The two partial exceptions are:
  1. **World Athletics certified-product lists.** Snippets of the official PDFs were seen, but the PDFs were not opened:
     - Surfacing list dated 2 Sep 2025: https://worldathletics.org/download/download?filename=c5cc2f26-2080-4b72-9ce1-734d89023418.pdf&urlslug=CERTIFICATES+-+Certified+Track+Surfacing+Products

  2. **FIBA Equipment & Venue partner pages** (about.fiba.basketball). These show licence numbers and levels.

### 1.3 Binder chemistry notes for buyers
- Standard Chinese track binders are **1-K moisture-cure MDI prepolymers** (aromatic). TDI-containing binders exist but are less favoured: MDI is cheaper and safer, though it has a shorter pot life of about 1–1.2 h gel (patent background: https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/7005477).
- **Aromatic binders yellow under UV.** Specify **aliphatic** binder for light-coloured EPDM surfaces (https://www.jiadongworld.com/blog-Playground/Aliphatic-pu-binder-create-non-yellowing-safety-epdm-surfaces-11990012.html).
- Made-in-China binder listings show roughly **US$1.50–2.35/kg**, MOQ 100 kg–1 t (https://www.made-in-china.com/manufacturers/polyurethane-binder.html). These are indicative list prices only.
- Ask for the following:
  - GB 36246-2018 test report (China's mandatory standard for synthetic-surface school fields)
  - Free-TDI/MDI and VOC data
  - SGS REACH/PAH reports
  - NCO %, viscosity, and gel/tack-free time at 35–45 °C for the Karachi climate

### 2.1 Specs to request (no verified spec sheets were obtained)
For every PVC court floor (badminton, volleyball, multi-sport, pickleball), ask for an **EN 14904 test report** from an accredited lab (e.g. Labosport, ISA-Sport, SGS) covering:
- Shock absorption % (EN 14904 minimum is 25 %)
- Vertical deformation
- Ball rebound (≥ 90 %)
- Friction/slip (80–110)
- Fire rating (EN 13501-1 Bfl-s1)

Also ask for:
- BWF/FIBA/FIVB/ITTF approval number for the exact product code
- Total thickness and wear-layer thickness
- Backing type (foam, felt, fabric)
- Roll width and length
- m² per 40HQ
- MOQ and warranty

No public FOB prices for PVC sports flooring were captured in this research. **Do not quote any.**

### 2.2 Clusters seen (flooring)
- **Hebei (Shijiazhuang):** PVC and PP-tile brands (Enlio, Geetkey, Jinta). The densest FIBA-licensed cluster found. It is **not** on the current trip route.
