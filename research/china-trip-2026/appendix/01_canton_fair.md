## 6. Practical logistics (task 5): mostly NOT verified this session
Everything below is general knowledge from before this session (LOW confidence). Recheck it on the official buyer site (buyer.cantonfair.org.cn / cantonfair.org.cn → "Buyer Registration").
- **Badge:** overseas buyers pre-register online on the official buyer platform: passport scan and photo, company details, invitation if a visa is needed. They then collect the buyer badge (a reusable IC card valid across sessions) at registration counters, or at the designated hotels and airport counters, by showing their passport. For pre-registered overseas buyers the badge has generally been free. A fee has applied to some replacements and on-site cases. Not verified for 2026.
- **Pakistan:** Pakistan is not on China's unilateral visa-free list as far as is known (unverified). Pakistani buyers usually need an M (business) visa. The fair's online platform can issue a visa invitation letter after registration. Apply early; Phase 2 is 8 days away.
- **Metro:** Line 8 stations Xingangdong (新港东, Area A), Pazhou (琶洲, Area B/C) and Pazhou Tower area; the Haizhu tram also runs along the complex. Line 8 links to Kecun (Line 3) and Changgang. LOW-MED.
- **Hotel areas:** Pazhou (walking distance, most expensive), Kecun/Chigang (one to two Line 8 stops), Zhujiang New Town / Tianhe (short taxi or metro ride). Book early because prices peak during the phases.
- **Tips:** arrive day 1 or 2 for the widest exhibitor presence (some stands leave early on the last day). Carry business cards and a WeChat/Alipay setup that accepts foreign cards. Photograph booth number boards. Use the gap days for factory visits (Foshan/Guangzhou for building materials, Shenzhen for electronics/LED, Dongguan/Jiangsu for turf/flooring).
- Not researched: fee amounts, 2026 badge rule changes, any Pakistan-specific buyer programme.
