## 0. Critical timing flags (read first)

1. **Visa is the critical path.**
   - Pakistan is **not** on China's 240-hour visa-free transit list, and it is not on the unilateral 30-day visa-free list. [S] [shanghai.gov.cn 240h policy](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-VisaLatest/20260709/d70a1231ba4641ff9a024e53751ad480.html), [chinatravel.com 240h guide](https://chinatravel.com/guide/visa/240-hour-visa-free-in-china)
   - Today is Oct 15 and Phase 2 opens Oct 23, so an **M visa must be applied for immediately**. Use the express service if it is offered.
   - Normal processing at the Chinese Visa Application Service Center in Karachi is about 4 working days, and express is about 2–3 days. [E: not confirmed; the site was unreachable]
   - If no visa is already in hand, Route (c) or (d) are the realistic options.
2. **Thailand visa.** Pakistani passport holders are, as far as I know, **not** visa-exempt for Thailand. They need a Thai e-Visa, which takes several working days. [E: verify at thaievisa.go.th] Apply in parallel with the China visa. Also fill in the Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) before arrival.
3. **Hong Kong.** Pakistanis generally need a visa or pre-arrival permission for Hong Kong. [E] Avoid routing through HKG, and avoid exiting to HK and re-entering China, unless you hold a double- or multiple-entry China visa. Ask for **double/multiple entry** on the M visa anyway.
4. **Canton Fair hotel surge.** Pazhou rates are reported at 2–3x the normal price during phases. Book now, or base outside Pazhou (see §4).

### 1.2 China business (M) visa for Pakistani citizens
Required documents, from the Chinese Consulate General Karachi checklist via visaforchina.cn [S] ([visaforchina KHI](https://www.visaforchina.cn/KHI2_EN/generalinformation/news/283468.shtml), [contact/notice](https://bio.visaforchina.cn/KHI3_EN/tongzhigonggao/333483543298707456.html)):
- Passport with at least 6 months of validity and blank pages, the application form, and a photo.
- **An invitation letter (or Verification Letter of Invitation / TE) from a Chinese entity.** A **Canton Fair invitation letter is accepted as the invitation.** [S] [wanderinchina Canton Fair visa guide](https://www.wanderinchina.com/destinations/guangzhou/canton-fair/visa/)
- A supporting letter from your company, the company's registration, **3 years of tax certificates**, and **6 months of bank statements**.
- Biometrics in person at the visa center. The officer may ask for an interview or extra documents.

Practical points:
- **Get a Canton Fair invitation.** Register online in the Canton Fair Buyer Service System and download the e-invitation.
  - A third-party blog says free invitations for first-time buyers have tightened. [S] [ikkyinchina](https://ikkyinchina.com/canton-fair-2026-invitation-letter-guide/)
  - Back it up with a **private invitation from an existing supplier** (company letterhead, business licence copy, trip dates, and who pays).
- There is no special "Canton Fair visa". A standard M visa is used. [S] [wanderinchina](https://www.wanderinchina.com/destinations/guangzhou/canton-fair/visa/)
- **Processing time:** no official figure was found. Assume about 4 working days for normal processing and 2–3 for express [E]. Also allow 1–2 days to gather the tax and bank documents.
- **Request double or multiple entry.** It costs little extra and protects you if you route via HK or Macau.

- Kunshan: about 30 min

- If **Nantong/Rugao** is a full day, consider one night in Nantong. Otherwise do it as a long day trip from Shanghai by rail (1h05) or from Wuxi by car.

## 6. Practical notes
- **Canton Fair phase content** [E] (check against the official cantonfair.org.cn listing and the other research files):
  - Phase 2 (Oct 23–27) covers consumer goods, gifts, home decor, and **building and decorative materials (flooring)**.
  - Phase 3 (Oct 31–Nov 4) covers textiles, shoes, office supplies, cases, food, health, and **sports, recreation and fitness products**.
- **Payments:**
  - Alipay and WeChat Pay accept foreign Visa and Mastercard cards without a Chinese bank account.
  - Payments up to about ¥200 have no fee. Above that a **3% fee** applies.
  - Per-transaction and annual limits conflict across sources (about ¥5,000–35,000 per payment, up to about ¥50,000–500,000 per year after advanced ID verification as of a reported May 2026 change). [S] [realchinatrip](https://realchinatrip.com/blogs/tips/alipay-for-foreigners-how-to-pay-in-china-2026-updated-realchinatrip), [ringgitplus](https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/apps/chinas-alipay-wechat-pay-now-accept-selected-international-credit-cards-including-visa-mastercard.html)
  - Set both apps up in Karachi before departure. Carry some RMB cash and a USD backup.
  - Pakistani-issued cards sometimes fail on foreign e-wallets, so carry two cards from different banks. [E]
- **Connectivity:**
  - A roaming or international travel eSIM (for example Holafly, Airalo, or a Hong Kong-routed plan) bypasses the firewall because traffic exits abroad. A Chinese local SIM does not, and needs a VPN.
  - **Install any VPN before arrival.** Keep the Pakistani SIM on roaming for SMS codes. [S] [yesim blog](https://blog.yesim.tech/?p=41202), [trip.com VPN guide](https://au.trip.com/guide/info/best-vpn-for-china.html)
- **Rides:** use DiDi in English (the international version accepts foreign cards), or via the Alipay mini-program. Amap (Gaode) has an English mode for navigation. [E] For factory days, hire a car with a driver at about ¥600–1,000 per day. [E]
- **Weather:**
  - **Guangzhou, late October:** highs about 27–29°C, lows about 20–21°C, about 4–7 rain days per month. Humid, so wear light business clothes.   - **Shanghai and Jiangsu, late Oct to early Nov:** highs about 19–23°C, lows about 13–16°C, cooling into November. Bring a light jacket. ## 7. Route sequences compared

Fixed dates: Phase 2 runs Oct 23–27 and Phase 3 runs Oct 31–Nov 4. The gap is **Oct 28–30 (3 days)**. Every route ends with about 2 days in BKK, then TG341 to KHI.

"Domestic transit" counts door-to-door travel inside China, plus the China→BKK leg. International KHI→China is about 11–14h on all routes.

### (a) KHI → CAN for Phase 2 → Jiangsu in the gap → CAN for Phase 3 → Shenzhen → BKK
| Date | Plan |
|---|---|
| Oct 21 | TG342 KHI 23:30 |
| Oct 22 | BKK → CAN, arrive midday. Hotel GZ (Kecun) |
| Oct 23–27 | Phase 2, 3–4 days, flooring and building materials. Day-trip Foshan if needed |
| Oct 27 eve | Fly CAN → WUX (about 2h20, door-to-door about 5h). Hotel Wuxi |
| Oct 28 | Changzhou Wujin/Henglin plus Wuxi |
| Oct 29 | Nantong/Rugao by car via the Jiangyin bridge, or Suzhou/Kunshan |
| Oct 30 | Fly WUX/SHA → CAN, door-to-door about 5h. Hotel GZ |
| Oct 31–Nov 2 | Phase 3 (fitness and sports) |
| Nov 2 eve | HSR to Shenzhen (about 1.5h door-to-door). Hotel Bao'an/Longhua |
| Nov 3 | Shenzhen/Dongguan factories |
| Nov 4 | SZX → BKK |
| Nov 4–6 | BKK |
| Nov 6 | TG341 BKK → KHI |

- **Hotels:** GZ → Wuxi → GZ → SZ → BKK. That is **4 changes (5 hotels)**.
- **Domestic transit:** about 5 + 5 + 1.5 + 5 (SZX→BKK including airport time) + about 4h of Jiangsu driving = **about 20h**.
- **Pros:** covers both phases plus Jiangsu flooring and Nantong fitness. It uses the 3-day gap productively.
- **Cons:** the visa must be ready by about Oct 20, which is very tight. It has the most moves, two peak-rate GZ stays, and is tiring. Nantong and Changzhou on one day is not feasible, so pick one per day.

### (b) KHI → Shanghai/Jiangsu first → CAN Phase 2 → Shenzhen/Dongguan in the gap → Phase 3 → BKK
| Date | Plan |
|---|---|
| Oct 18–19 | KHI → PVG via BKK (TG) or DXB |
| Oct 19–22 | Jiangsu, Wuxi base: Changzhou, Nantong, Suzhou. Optional Ningjin (about 2 extra days; HSR Shanghai → Dezhou East about 4h10) |
| Oct 22 | Fly WUX/SHA → CAN |
| Oct 23–27 | Phase 2 |
| Oct 28–30 | Shenzhen/Dongguan |
| Oct 30 eve | Back to GZ |
| Oct 31–Nov 2 | Phase 3 |
| Nov 3 | CAN → BKK |
| Nov 3–5 | BKK, then home |

- **Hotels:** Wuxi → GZ → SZ → GZ → BKK. That is **4 changes**, or 3 if you keep the GZ hotel through the gap and day-trip Shenzhen/Dongguan.
- **Domestic transit:** about 5 (Jiangsu → CAN) + 1.5 + 1.5 + 4 (CAN→BKK) + about 4h of Jiangsu driving = **about 16h**, or **about 26h with Ningjin**.
- **Pros:** most complete, and the only route where Ningjin fits. It lets you meet factories before the fair, so you arrive with price benchmarks.
- **Cons:** **not feasible on visa timing.** You would need a visa by about Oct 17. It is also the longest trip (about 18–19 days) and the most expensive.

### (c) Phase 3 only: arrive Oct 30 → CAN → Shenzhen → BKK
| Date | Plan |
|---|---|
| Oct 29 | TG342 KHI 23:30 |
| Oct 30 | Arrive CAN midday |
| Oct 31–Nov 2 | Phase 3 (fitness and sports) |
| Nov 2 eve | HSR to Shenzhen |
| Nov 3–4 | Shenzhen/Dongguan factories |
| Nov 5 | SZX/CAN → BKK |

- **Hotels:** GZ → SZ → BKK. That is **2 changes**.
- **Domestic transit:** about 1.5 + 5 = **about 6.5h**.
- **Pros:** simplest and cheapest. It gives 2 extra weeks for the visa and Thai e-Visa. It is focused on fitness, which is in Phase 3.
- **Cons:** misses Phase 2 (flooring and building materials) and the Jiangsu flooring and Nantong free-weights clusters.

### (d) Phase 2 last 2 days + Phase 3 first 2 days, with Shenzhen/Dongguan in the gap
| Date | Plan |
|---|---|
| Oct 25 | Arrive CAN (TG342 departs Oct 24 23:30) |
| Oct 26–27 | Phase 2, flooring and building materials. Last days, often with discounts and samples |
| Oct 28 | HSR to Shenzhen. Hotel Longhua/Bao'an |
| Oct 28–30 | Shenzhen plus Dongguan (Humen and Chang'an about 20 min HSR or 45 min drive). Foshan possible on Oct 30 on the way back |
| Oct 30 eve | Back to GZ |
| Oct 31–Nov 1 | Phase 3 |
| Nov 2 | CAN → BKK |
| Nov 2–4 | BKK |
| Nov 4 | TG341 BKK → KHI |

- **Hotels:** GZ → SZ → GZ → BKK. That is **3 changes**, or **1 change** if you keep one GZ hotel (Kecun, Line 3/8, 30 min HSR to Shenzhen) and day-trip Shenzhen/Dongguan.
- **Domestic transit:** about 1.5 + 1.5 + 4 = **about 7h**, or about 3h/day of commuting if you day-trip.
- **Pros:** sees both phases' key halls and uses the gap on the Pearl River Delta supply base. It allows a 10-day visa window. It is about 11 days total.
- **Cons:** no Jiangsu, so Changzhou flooring and Nantong weights are left for supplier video calls or a later trip. Late Phase 2 means some exhibitors have already packed up.
- **Variant (d+):** swap Shenzhen for a 2-day Jiangsu hop.
  - Fly CAN → WUX on Oct 27 evening, do Changzhou on Oct 28 and Nantong on Oct 29, fly back Oct 30.
  - This is Route (a) compressed. Transit is about 12h, with 3 changes.
