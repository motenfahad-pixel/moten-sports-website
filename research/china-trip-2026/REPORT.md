## 1. Executive summary

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Attend Phase 2 (23–27 Oct)?** | **No, by default.** The official 140th section table puts **"Sports, Travel & Recreation Products"** in **Phase 3**. Phase 2's flooring is *building and decorative* flooring (SPC/LVT/laminate). Where we found sports-flooring and turf exhibitors from past sessions, they sat in the **Phase 3 sports halls**: Dongxing/Shengbang PVC sports floor at **12.1J28** in Phase 3 of the 138th, and CityGreen turf/padel at **13.1 D43-44** at the 139th. PU binders count as "new materials & chemicals", which is **Phase 1**. **Exception:** if your exhibitor search finds **3 or more verified, relevant** sports-surface or track exhibitors in Phase 2, spend **one** day there. |
| **Attend Phase 3 (31 Oct–4 Nov)?** | **Yes, for 3 days (31 Oct–2 Nov).** This phase covers the gym tender, sports flooring, turf and padel, ball machines, court accessories, rackets and paddles. Days 4–5 add little: exhibitors start leaving, and you will have more value in Shenzhen. |
| **Is Yiwu worth it?** | **No.** It is a trader market for generic, low-value accessories. Nothing strategic for ScoreX is sold there: no ball machines, cameras or scoreboards. Branded padel and pickleball items are better bought factory-direct in Guangzhou or Hangzhou, or found at Phase 3. If you need cheap accessories later, use a Yiwu buying agent. |
| **Days in Shenzhen?** | **2 days, plus half a day in Dongguan for ball machines.** Day 1: Longhua camera cluster. Day 2: Nanshan edge-AI and IP-camera cluster. |
| **Days in Jiangsu?** | **0 in the recommended plan, or 2 days (variant B+)** if your existing Jiangsu factory needs an in-person visit this trip. Jiangsu's verified sports targets are Wuxi (Boker/DMD PVC sports floor), Nantong (free weights and Topflor flooring) and CCGrass turf in Huai'an/Nanjing. They are useful but not critical, and **a Ningjin visit gives more value for the gym tender.** |
| **Days in Shandong (Ningjin)?** | **2 days, inside the 28–30 Oct gap.** Ningjin county (Dezhou) is China's commercial-gym cluster: about 2,500 firms, which state media says make about 70% of China's domestic fitness equipment. DHZ, MND and MBH are there. Most of these big makers sell through FIBO and IWF, not Canton Fair, so the fair will **not** reliably put them in front of you. |
| **Can the trip be shortened?** | **Yes.** The **9-day short plan** (§14) keeps Phase 3, Guangzhou track suppliers, Dongguan ball machines and Shenzhen cameras. It drops Ningjin and the Qingyuan factories. Choose it if the gym tender is not live by departure or your visa slips. |
| **Recommended total trip length** | **15 days door to door (25 Oct night → 8 Nov):** 10 nights in China and 3 in Bangkok. That is **Option B** (§13). |

**The surprise in the data:** your track-material priority (A) is concentrated in **Guangdong**, not Jiangsu. JRace (Guangzhou plus a Yingde factory, FIBA Level-1 licensed), Tongxin (Guangzhou, prefabricated track), Chuanao (Qingyuan, EPDM/SBR granules) and Like New Materials (binder, EPDM, silicon PU) are all near the fair. That is why the 28–30 Oct gap goes to Guangdong and Shandong, and Jiangsu drops out.

**Critical-path risk: your visa.** Pakistan is **not** eligible for China's visa-free transit. You need an **M visa** with an invitation (a Canton Fair e-invitation or a supplier letter). If you do not have the visa by about **23 Oct**, use the short plan (§14). You also need a **Thai e-Visa**. Apply for both today.

**Key findings for MDS**
1. **Binder chemistry** comes from Zibo, Shandong (INOV, Century Union). Wanhua is the upstream MDI producer, **not** a track-binder brand you would buy from; that is our inference. Listed binder prices run about **US$1.5–2.35/kg**, at MOQs from 100 kg to 1 t. For light-coloured EPDM, specify **aliphatic** binder; aromatic binder yellows under UV.
2. **Turnkey systems you can visit on this trip:** JRace, Tongxin and Like, all in Guangdong.
3. **World Athletics-listed Chinese prefabricated and full-PUR tracks** cluster in **Hebei and Zhejiang**: Huadong (Wenzhou), Hangzhou Da Niu, Hengshui Fanya, plus Shanghai Headway. Handle them by video call, and visit only if a WA-certified track tender appears.
4. **No TPE-granule maker was identified.** Ask JRace and Chuanao.
5. **No Pakistan or Middle East export evidence** was found for any track supplier. Ask for Gulf and South Asia references.
6. Download the **current WA certified-surfaces list**. It was updated 1 Oct 2026. Check product names and certificate numbers before trusting any "IAAF certified" claim.

## 16. Questions to ask suppliers

### Athletic track supplier
1. Which of these do you **manufacture yourself**: binder, EPDM, SBR, TPE, prefabricated sheet? Which do you buy in? Show me the reactor or mixing line, and the granule or calender line.
2. Is the binder aromatic or aliphatic, MDI or TDI? What are the NCO %, viscosity, and pot life and tack-free time at 35–45 °C (Karachi)?
3. Give me the World Athletics certificate number and product name exactly as listed, the date and the test lab. Do you have a GB 36246-2018 report?
4. What systems do you supply: full PU, sandwich, spray coat, self-knitting/breathable, prefabricated? What are the thickness and the material consumption (kg/m²) for each layer?
5. What are the price per m² of system and per kg or tonne of each component, and the FOB port? What are the MOQ and quantity per 20GP/40HQ? What is the shelf life of the binder?
6. Do you install? Will you send a supervisor to Pakistan? What do you charge per day? Do you offer training and certification of the MDS crew?
7. What warranty do you give (years, UV and colour fade, delamination)? What maintenance plan do you recommend?
8. Give me references for the Middle East or South Asia, with contactable clients. Do you have an existing Pakistan agent? Is exclusivity available?
9. Will you share the REACH/PAH, VOC and free-isocyanate reports and an MSDS for each chemical? How is the binder classified for sea freight?

### Flooring supplier
1. For each product code, what are the total thickness, wear layer, backing and surface treatment (UV/PU, anti-slip texture)?
2. Do you have an EN 14904 report covering shock absorption, vertical deformation, ball rebound and friction? From which lab, and dated when?
3. What fire rating do you hold (EN 13501-1 Bfl-s1)? What are the UV class and outdoor suitability?
4. Which BWF, FIBA, FIVB or ITTF approval numbers apply to **this exact code**?
5. For pickleball: what is the ball-bounce consistency on tiles versus acrylic? Do you have USA Pickleball-compatible surfaces?
6. What are the roll size, m² per 40HQ, MOQ, colours, lead time and price per m² FOB?
7. Do you supply adhesive, a line-marking system and an installation manual? What subfloor do you require?
8. What warranty do you give, and do you have Pakistan or Gulf references? Is exclusivity available?

### Gym equipment manufacturer
1. Do you hold EN ISO 20957 test reports (Parts 1, 2 and 6 for treadmills)? Do you have a CE DoC under the Machinery Directive, ISO 9001 (issuer), and TÜV or SGS reports?
2. Is this an **own factory or outsourced**? Which processes are in-house: frame welding, powder coat, upholstery, motor/electronics?
3. Treadmills: continuous HP, maximum user weight, 230 V/50 Hz, deck and belt life.
4. Strength: weight-stack tolerance, cable spec, steel gauge, upholstery grade.
5. What warranty do you give for the frame, parts, electronics and wear items? Do you supply a spare-parts kit (2–5% of order value)? Do you offer remote technician support or training?
6. Do you offer full package quotes (cardio, strength, free weights, functional, Pilates, flooring and lockers), and will you do a free 3D layout for the tender?
7. Give me the MOQ, lead time, crate or carton packing, the 40HQ loading plan and the FOB port.
8. Is there an existing Pakistan distributor? Will you grant written exclusivity, and what are the volume targets? Can you share Middle East project references?
9. Do you do OEM or private label? What are the logo-on-frame MOQ and the console branding options?

### Ball machine manufacturer
1. Will you give the **BLE GATT map and full command protocol** under NDA? What is the protocol version policy?
2. Can we install **custom firmware or OTA through our app**? Who owns the firmware? Will you provide source or a binary escrow?
3. Is there a Wi-Fi or Ethernet variant? Which MCU and BT module do you use? Do you have FCC ID and CE-RED?
4. What are the motor type and life, the speed range, spin modes, oscillation, elevation and feed interval? What are the ball capacity and the balls supported (tennis, padel, pickleball)?
5. Battery: chemistry, Wh, UN38.3 and MSDS. Do you also have a mains option?
6. Custom enclosure, colour and logo: what are the MOQ and the mould or NRE fee?
7. What are the warranty, spare parts (wheels, boards, motors) and repair guide? What are the lead time and the price at 50, 100 and 300 units?
8. Will you give ScoreX exclusivity for Pakistan, the Middle East or South Asia?

### Camera manufacturer
1. Which sensor, shutter type (global or rolling), resolution and maximum fps do you offer at 4K and 1080p?
2. Encoding: H.264/H.265, bitrate/fps control, glass-to-glass latency on RTSP. Do you support SRT?
3. Do you support **ONVIF profiles**, an RTSP API, and an **SDK (Linux/ARM)**? Do you have a web API for exposure, shutter, white balance and gain?
4. How do you handle **time sync**: NTP, PTP (IEEE 1588), or a hardware trigger input? Is there a frame timestamp in the stream metadata?
5. What PoE class and IP rating do you offer? What is the operating temperature (45 °C or more for Karachi summers)? What housing and lens options (FOV) are available?
6. Can you do custom firmware, logo, housing or PCB? What are the NRE and MOQ?
7. Certifications: CE, FCC, RoHS. How long is the product lifecycle and supply commitment (5 years or more)?
8. What are the price at 50, 200 and 1,000 units, the sample cost and the lead time?

### Electronics manufacturer
1. What do you do: PCB, PCBA, box-build, enclosure (injection or CNC), cable harness?
2. Do you offer DFM review and component sourcing? How do you substitute parts and lock the BOM?
3. Testing: AOI, X-ray, ICT/functional test. Will you build a test jig to our spec?
4. Quality: IPC class, ISO 9001, traceability, RMA process.
5. What are the MOQ, NRE, prototype-to-production lead times and payment terms?
6. Will you protect our IP: NDA, no design reuse, and return of tooling ownership?
